Our Mission

To make on-chain compliance as fundamental to Web3 as SSL is to the internet. Every transaction should be screened, every policy should be deterministic, and every rule should be auditable — without sacrificing decentralization.

Values

What we believe

Determinism Over Trust

Compliance rules should execute the same way every time, for every transaction. No hidden logic, no manual exceptions.

Transparency Over Opacity

All policies, risk profiles, and screening decisions are on-chain and publicly auditable. Regulators can verify; users can trust.

Decentralization Over Control

We don't hold your keys, freeze your funds, or censor your transactions. The protocol enforces rules; humans don't.
