Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

FidesOrigin is an on-chain compliance protocol. By design, we minimize data collection. This policy explains what limited data we handle and how we protect it.

2. Information We Collect

On-chain data: All risk assessments and compliance checks happen on-chain. Transaction data is public by nature of blockchain technology and is not collected by us.

Website analytics: We use minimal analytics to understand website usage. This may include IP address (anonymized), browser type, and pages visited.

Wallet connections: When you connect a wallet, we only read your public address. We never request transaction signing or access to private keys.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve our services
  • To respond to support requests
  • To analyze website usage patterns
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies for website functionality. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Vercel: Website hosting
  • The Graph: Blockchain data indexing
  • RPC Providers: Blockchain node access

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures. However, no system is completely secure. Users are responsible for securing their own wallet private keys.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@fidesorigin.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@fidesorigin.com.
