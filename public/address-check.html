How It Works

On-Chain. Immutable. Real-Time.

01

Submit Address

Enter any Ethereum address. No wallet connection required — all queries are public view calls.

02

Guard Pre-Check

The V2.1 Guard engine queries the on-chain RiskRegistry for risk scores, tiers, and sanctions.

03

Verdict Delivered

Results returned directly from the smart contract: CLEAR, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, or CRITICAL risk.
