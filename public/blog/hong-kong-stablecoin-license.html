Hong Kong Stablecoin License: Compliance Requirements

July 2026 Regulation 10 min read

Hong Kong has emerged as Asia's premier hub for regulated digital assets. The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) introduced its stablecoin issuer licensing regime in 2024, setting a high bar for compliance that goes beyond typical VASP requirements.

The HKMA Licensing Framework

Under the new regime, any entity issuing fiat-referenced stablecoins (FRS) in Hong Kong or actively marketing such stablecoins to Hong Kong residents must obtain a license from the HKMA. The requirements are stringent:

  • Local Presence: Must incorporate in Hong Kong with a physical office
  • Capital Requirements: Minimum paid-up capital of HK$25 million
  • Reserve Assets: High-quality liquid assets held in segregated accounts with licensed banks
  • Redemption: At par value within one business day
  • Disclosure: Regular attestation and audit reports

On-Chain Compliance Requirements

What makes Hong Kong's regime unique is its emphasis on real-time monitoring and reporting. Licensed issuers must demonstrate:

  1. Transaction Screening: All transfers must be screened against sanctions lists before execution
  2. Wallet Monitoring: Continuous monitoring of all wallets holding the stablecoin
  3. Suspicious Activity Reporting: Automatic flagging and reporting to the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit
  4. Travel Rule Compliance: VASP-to-VASP information exchange for transfers over HK$8,000
"The HKMA expects compliance to be embedded in the protocol itself, not bolted on as an afterthought."

Technical Implementation

FidesOrigin's on-chain compliance engine directly addresses these requirements:

  • Deterministic Screening: Every transfer is evaluated against on-chain risk profiles. No API dependency means no downtime.
  • Audit Trail: All screening decisions are logged on-chain, providing immutable evidence for regulators.
  • Real-Time Updates: Risk profiles are updated via Chainlink Functions, ensuring the latest sanctions data is always available.
  • Quarantine Mechanism: Suspicious transfers are held in escrow rather than blocked, allowing for review without disrupting legitimate users.

The Road Ahead

With Hong Kong positioning itself as a global crypto hub, the licensing regime is expected to become even more comprehensive. Early movers who build compliance into their protocols from day one will have a significant advantage.

FidesOrigin provides the infrastructure to meet these requirements deterministically, transparently, and without compromising on the decentralization that makes blockchain technology valuable.
