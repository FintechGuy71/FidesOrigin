Process

From Intent to Execution

Three layers. Zero gaps. Every transaction checked before it touches the blockchain.

01

Pre-Flight Simulation

Guard's previewTransaction() runs a staticcall against RiskRegistry and PolicyEngine — simulating the full compliance pipeline with zero gas cost and zero state mutation.

02

Risk & Policy Evaluation

20,000+ address profiles checked in milliseconds. Sanctions lists, mixer associations, transaction velocity, geo-fencing, and KYC tier — all evaluated deterministically.

03

On-Chain Enforcement

CLEAR → transfer executes. BLOCK → transaction reverts before any state change. QUARANTINE → funds routed to isolated vault with immutable audit log.
