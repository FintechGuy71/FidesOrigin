Business Model

Why Per-Transaction Beats Annual SaaS

The compliance industry charges you whether you use the service or not. We believe infrastructure should cost less as you optimize.

Pay for What You Use

Annual SaaS subscriptions cost $50K–$500K regardless of transaction volume. With per-transaction pricing, you only pay when transactions are screened. No usage, no bill.

Economies of Scale

As your transaction volume grows, your effective per-transaction cost drops. Volume discounts kick in automatically at 100K+ transactions/month. The more you grow, the more you save.

Zero Hidden Fees

No setup fees, no overage charges, no surprise invoices. The price you see is the price you pay: 10¢–50¢ per transaction + a small percentage of value. Fully transparent.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the billing work?
You are billed monthly based on actual transactions screened. Starter plan: 10¢ per transaction after the first 10,000 free transactions each month, plus 0.05% of transaction value. Professional plan: 25¢ per transaction after the first 50,000 free, plus 0.02% of value. Enterprise plans are custom-negotiated. No annual contracts required.
Which blockchains are supported?
FidesOrigin currently supports Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, and Avalanche C-Chain. Starter plans include Ethereum and Base mainnet. Professional and Enterprise plans unlock all supported networks. Custom chain integrations are available for Enterprise clients.
Can I create custom compliance policies?
Yes. Professional plans include pre-built policy templates (KYC/AML, transaction limits, geo-fencing) that can be customized via our Policy Engine. Enterprise plans offer fully bespoke policy strategies developed with your compliance team and deployed as on-chain smart contract modules.
How is my data secured?
All compliance decisions are recorded immutably on-chain with cryptographic proofs. No sensitive customer data is stored off-chain. Our smart contracts have undergone multi-round third-party security audits. Enterprise plans include optional on-premise deployment for organizations with strict data residency requirements.
What happens when I exceed my free tier?
Transactions continue to be processed seamlessly. You are billed for the overage at your plan's per-transaction rate at the end of the billing cycle. We never block transactions due to billing. Volume discounts are applied automatically when you consistently exceed 100,000 transactions per month.
Can I switch plans at any time?
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade between Starter and Professional plans at any time. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Enterprise contracts have custom terms negotiated with your account manager.
