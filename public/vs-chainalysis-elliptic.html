Four Fundamental Differences

What Sets FidesOrigin Apart

Same industry, different layer of the stack. Four dimensions where architecture and timing create entirely different outcomes.

Timing

Real-Time Interception

Chainalysis and Elliptic analyze transactions after confirmation for investigation. FidesOrigin evaluates and blocks before execution, preventing loss rather than documenting it.

Execution Layer

Smart Contract Native

Traditional tools live off-chain, providing data for human decisions. FidesOrigin embeds enforcement logic in smart contracts, making compliance deterministic and non-bypassable.

Pricing

Pay Per Transaction

Annual SaaS subscriptions ($50K–$500K) price out smaller players. Per-transaction pricing (10–50¢) scales with usage and eliminates upfront commitment.

Openness

MIT Licensed, Audited

Closed-source compliance creates opacity risk. FidesOrigin's full source is MIT licensed with multi-round third-party audits. Regulators and auditors can verify every line.

"The fundamental shift is from 'here's what happened' to 'this transaction will not execute.' That difference is the gap between compliance theater and real risk prevention."

— Protocol Design Note, FidesOrigin v2.4.1

Use Case Comparison

When Each Solution Fits

No tool is universally best. The right choice depends on your risk model, budget, and regulatory requirements.

01

Stablecoin Issuer Needs HKMA License

A Hong Kong-based stablecoin issuer must demonstrate real-time AML screening, immutable audit trails, and automated redemption controls to HKMA examiners.

FidesOrigin

Pluggable compliance module embeds directly in stablecoin contract. Every transfer is screened in <50ms. All decisions logged immutably on-chain. Audit trail is cryptographic, not a database export. One issuer achieved HKMA in-principle approval using FidesOrigin.

Chainalysis / Elliptic

Provide risk data via API. Issuer must build custom integration, store audit logs in a database, and prove to regulators that every screening call was actually made. No on-chain enforcement means compliance is only as good as the integration code.

02

DeFi Protocol Wants Institutional Capital

An on-chain lending protocol needs to attract institutional LPs who require compliance guarantees before depositing funds.

FidesOrigin

Guard module integrated at the protocol level. Every borrow/lend/liquidation checked against policy. Institutional LPs can verify compliance logic on-chain. No black box — the policy is the code.

Chainalysis / Elliptic

Protocol queries risk API at the UI level. A sophisticated user can bypass the UI and interact directly with the smart contract, never triggering the risk check. Compliance theater, not enforcement.

Decision Framework

When to Choose What

Honest guidance: each solution has its ideal use case.

Choose FidesOrigin when you need:

  • ✓ Real-time transaction interception (not just detection)
  • ✓ On-chain, immutable audit trails for regulators
  • ✓ Per-transaction pricing (no annual commitment)
  • ✓ Open-source infrastructure you can audit and modify
  • ✓ Stablecoin or RWA tokenization requiring license-grade compliance
  • ✓ Zero-confirmation mempool risk interception

Choose Chainalysis / Elliptic when you need:

  • ✓ Investigative forensics for law enforcement
  • ✓ Historical transaction tracing and graph analysis
  • ✓ Threat intelligence and darknet monitoring
  • ✓ Large enterprise with dedicated compliance team
  • ✓ Budget for $100K+ annual SaaS commitment

Many organizations use both: Chainalysis/Elliptic for investigation and intelligence, FidesOrigin for real-time enforcement.
